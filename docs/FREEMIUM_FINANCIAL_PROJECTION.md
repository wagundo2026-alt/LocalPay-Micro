# LocalPay Micro — revised freemium financial planning scenario

**Status:** illustrative hackathon forecast; not actual revenue, paid uptake, provider approval, or a guarantee. Updated 12 November 2026.

## New business model

- **Starter:** R0/month, up to 200 initiated checkout attempts monthly; QR and payment links, essential transaction records.
- **Basic:** R49/month, up to 500 initiated checkouts; optional, with enhanced business reporting proposed.
- **Pro:** R99/month, up to 1,000 initiated checkouts; optional reporting and tools.
- **Pro Plus:** R199/month, up to 5,000 initiated checkouts; optional high-volume tools.
- Payment-provider costs remain separate. No live subscriptions are charged and no real funds settle in the hackathon TEST environment.
- A *planned* eligible-merchant promotion of 1% processing costs for the first three months would be funded by a LocalPay subsidy **capped at R30 per merchant per month**. After the cap or period, standard disclosed provider fees apply. This promotion is **not implemented or approved**.

## Revised subscription-only illustrative scenario

Merchants below are **average accounts over the year**. Paid merchants are a subset, not all merchants.

| Metric | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Starter (R0) | 100 | 245 | 540 |
| Basic (R49) | 0 | 70 | 180 |
| Pro (R99) | 0 | 28 | 135 |
| Pro Plus (R199) | 0 | 7 | 45 |
| Total average merchants | 100 | 350 | 900 |
| Monthly subscription revenue | R0 | R7,595 | R31,140 |
| Annual subscription revenue | R0 | R91,140 | R373,680 |
| Illustrative operating costs (before promotion and other omitted costs) | R150,000 | R250,000 | R480,000 |
| Operating position before subsidies and omitted costs | -R150,000 | -R158,860 | -R106,320 |

**Formula:** annual subscription revenue = 12 × (Basic merchants × 49 + Pro merchants × 99 + Pro Plus merchants × 199).

**Promotion exposure:** R30/merchant/month × up to 3 months = maximum R90 subsidy per eligible merchant. A hypothetical pilot of 100 eligible merchants creates up to R9,000 subsidy liability over three months, before any other operational and compliance costs. Promotion eligibility, instrument costs and start dates have not been agreed.

**Do not use the earlier Year 1 R58,800 / Year 2 R289,200 / Year 3 R853,200 revenue forecast** alongside this freemium model: it assumes a different paid subscription mix and is inconsistent with Starter being free.

## What would be needed to reach profitability

The illustrative figures above show operating losses in all three years from subscriptions alone. The business needs a better paid conversion rate, lower expenses, permitted fee revenues or licensed institutional contracts; those must be validated instead of invented. Provider processing charges received on behalf of merchants are not LocalPay revenue. A transaction markup must be explicitly contracted and compatible with affordable-payment promises.

**Funding request:** R1,000,000 is the latest pitch ask, not money received. The funding budget should be updated and reconciled separately from operating costs rather than counted as subscription revenue.
